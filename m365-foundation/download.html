🚀 After Installation

1. Verify Installation

m365-pro --version m365ctl --help

2. Activate License

Download your license.json and license.sig from the Customer Portal

mkdir -p ~/.config/m365_webapps cp license.json ~/.config/m365_webapps/ cp license.sig ~/.config/m365_webapps/ # Verify m365-pro -feature picker \ -license ~/.config/m365_webapps/license.json \ -sig ~/.config/m365_webapps/license.sig

3. Launch Apps

Desktop launchers are automatically created! Look for:
