Business SSO and SCIM Setup

Use this checklist to connect Azure AD SSO, generate a SCIM token, and locate audit logs.

Setup

  1. Open the Azure AD SSO setup guide and configure the application for your tenant.
  2. Generate a SCIM token from the enterprise portal token page.
  3. Open the enterprise audit page to review login and provisioning events.
