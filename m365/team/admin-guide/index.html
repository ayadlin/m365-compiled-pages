Team Admin Guide

Use this checklist to install M365 WebApps, invite teammates, and add seats when your team grows.

Setup

  1. Install M365 WebApps and save the license files from your welcome email.
  2. Open the customer portal and invite teammates from the team administration flow.
  3. Use the seat-add CTA in the portal when you need additional team seats.

Support

Email support@ytech.tools with the purchase email and affected teammate address.
