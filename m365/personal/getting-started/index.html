Personal Getting Started

Use this checklist after purchase to install M365 WebApps and activate your Personal license.

Install

  1. Download M365 WebApps from the package channel for your Linux distribution.
  2. Follow the install guide in the documentation.
  3. Save license.json and license.sig in ~/.config/m365_webapps/.

Support

Email support@ytech.tools with your purchase email, Linux distribution, and exact error text.
